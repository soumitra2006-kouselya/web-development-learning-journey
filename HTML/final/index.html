Coconut chutney is a quintessential South Indian condiment celebrated for its rich, creamy texture and refreshing taste. Made primarily from freshly grated white coconut flesh, it is blended with green chilies for heat, roasted gram (pottukadalai) for thickness, and ginger for a subtle kick.The defining touch is the tadka (tempering): a fragrant mix of mustard seeds, split urad dal, dried red chilies, and fresh curry leaves spluttered in hot oil and poured on top. Universally paired with breakfast classics like idli, dosa, and vada, this versatile relish perfectly balances spicy, nutty, and savory flavors.
