The Weight of the Glass

A psychology professor walked into his lecture hall holding a half-empty glass of water. He raised it and asked the students, "How heavy is this glass?" Answers ranged from eight ounces to twenty ounces.The professor smiled and said, "The absolute weight does not matter. It depends on how long I hold it. If I hold it for a minute, it is light. If I hold it for an hour, my arm aches. If I hold it for a day, my arm grows numb. The weight never changes, but the longer I hold it, the heavier it feels."Life's worries are like that glass. Put them down sometimes.
